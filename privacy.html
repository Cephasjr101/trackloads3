Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · LoadMatch Ltd, Accra, Ghana · Contact: privacy@loadmatch.example.com

1. Who we are

LoadMatch operates a marketplace connecting truck owners with empty return capacity to shippers needing transportation. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone number, and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Verification data: for carriers — truck registration, plate number, driver licence / Ghana Card details — used solely to issue our Verified badge.
  • Listing data: routes, dates, capacity, cargo descriptions and budgets you submit.
  • Location data: GPS breadcrumbs during active shipments, visible to both parties to that shipment.
  • Payment data: processed by our payment partner; we never store full card numbers.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and usage data via analytics — only after you accept cookies.

3. How we use it

  • To match trucks and loads, display listings, and operate tracking, contracts, escrow and proof of delivery.
  • To verify carriers and prevent fraud.
  • To improve the platform (aggregated, de-identified analytics).
  • To meet legal, tax and regulatory obligations in Ghana.

4. Cookies

Essential cookies keep the site working. Analytics cookies load only if you click “Accept cookies” on the banner. You can withdraw consent anytime by clearing site data in your browser.

5. Sharing

We share the minimum needed with: the other party to your match (contact details, route, cargo), our payment processor, and authorities where legally required. We never sell personal data.

6. Retention & security

Shipment records are retained for 6 years for tax/audit purposes; GPS breadcrumbs are deleted 90 days after delivery. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.

7. Your rights

You may request access, correction, export or deletion of your personal data by emailing privacy@loadmatch.example.com. We respond within 30 days. You may complain to the Data Protection Commission, Ghana.
