Last updated: September 2026 · By using LoadMatch you accept these terms.

1. The service

LoadMatch is a marketplace that introduces verified carriers with empty return capacity to shippers. LoadMatch is not itself a carrier; the transport contract is between shipper and carrier and is formed by the digital contract generated on the platform.

2. Verification

Carriers must pass truck and driver verification before accepting cargo. Providing false documents terminates the account and voids platform protections.

3. Payments & commission

  • The shipper pays the agreed price into LoadMatch escrow before pickup.
  • LoadMatch deducts a commission of 3–10% per transaction, displayed on the digital contract before signing.
  • Funds are released to the carrier within 1 business day of verified proof of delivery (OTP confirmation).
  • Prices are quoted in Ghana Cedis (GH₵).

4. Shipper obligations

Accurate cargo declarations (weight, volume, nature). Prohibited cargo — illegal goods, hazardous materials without declaration — voids the contract and may be reported to authorities.

5. Carrier obligations

Collect and deliver within the contracted window, keep GPS sharing enabled during transit, and obtain the recipient's OTP code at delivery.

6. Liability

The carrier is liable for loss or damage to cargo in transit up to the declared value. LoadMatch's liability is limited to the commission it earned on the affected transaction. GPS and POD records are the primary evidence in disputes.

7. Disputes

Disputes are handled through LoadMatch support within 14 days of delivery, using contract, GPS and POD records. Unresolved disputes fall under the courts of Ghana.

8. Acceptable use & termination

No circumvention (exchanging contact details to avoid platform fees results in a GH₵500 penalty per Terms §8), no fraud, no spam. We may suspend accounts that breach these terms.
